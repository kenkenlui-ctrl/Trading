Privacy Policy

Leeks Terminal does not collect personal information. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking, no email collection.

Data Collection

None from us. We do not require account registration, do not place tracking cookies, and do not share user data with third parties.

Market Data Sources

All stock prices, indices, and capital-flow data displayed on this site come from public APIs:

  • Futu OpenD for HK quotes (sub-second delay)
  • Tencent qtimg for HK quote fallback
  • YFinance for US / end-of-day data
  • Futu Cloud News (free tier) for headlines

These are third-party services with their own terms and conditions.

Cookies and Analytics

We do not use analytics cookies. We do not track your IP address, browser fingerprint, or behavior. The site is static HTML served via Cloudflare Pages; Cloudflare's standard server logs are retained temporarily for abuse prevention and then discarded.

Third-Party Services

Hosted on Cloudflare Pages; fonts served by Google Fonts CDN; no other third-party trackers or advertising networks are used.

Data Retention

Daily market data is regenerated on T-1 close and overwrites prior day; no user-level history is kept. If you contact us via email, your message is retained only as long as needed to respond.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the site operator through the email listed on the About page.
