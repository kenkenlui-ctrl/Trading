Methodology

  • Universe: 121 BUY signals from 2026-06-27 to 2026-07-31 (T+10 swing horizon)
  • Initial capital: HK$1,000,000
  • Risk per trade: 0.5% of equity (position sized to stop distance)
  • Max concurrent: 10 positions; if full, signal skipped
  • Holding period: 10 trading days; exit at end OR at stop/target (already in return_pct)
  • Costs: HK 0.25% round-trip already deducted from return_pct by source backtest
  • Mark-to-market: daily equity = cash + sum(open position value at current return_pct)
  • CAGR: annualized from (final/initial)^(365/days) - 1
  • Max DD: peak-to-trough drawdown on daily equity

Limitations

  • No slippage or liquidity filter — return_pct is a backtest estimate
  • No shortable filter (T+10 strategy is long-only)
  • No corporate-action adjustment (splits/dividends)
  • Same "today's top-200 universe" used historically (selection bias, see methodology)
  • Single backtest run; no walk-forward / out-of-sample split (planned for d72)
