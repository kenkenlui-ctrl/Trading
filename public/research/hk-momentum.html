點樣讀呢個數

位移 ±21 / ±63 bars 係 placebo：將調倉日推前推後一個月或一個季度。 五個變體全部落喺 14.54%–15.83%，跨度 1.29 pp —— 窄區間代表結果對日期對齊唔敏感，唔係單一日期撞好運。

剔除 2020 年疫情同 2022 年熊市窗口後，年化由 15.11% 升至 16.07%。 即係唔係靠大事件賺錢，相反喺平靜期表現更好。

T-1 前視探針：28 隻成分喺 D+1 人為插入假尖峰後完全唔變（True）。

三個不能忽略的限制

1. 存續者偏誤。The universe is 174 CURRENT constituents. Stocks delisted, privatised or acquired since 2016-09-01 are absent, which inflates every figure here. Closing this gap needs point-in-time index membership, which this repository does not hold.

2. 這條規則是從 12 個組合裡選出來的。These parameters were chosen in a prior screening pass that compared 4 momentum descriptors x 3 rebalance frequencies = 12 combinations. This run tests ONE pre-registered rule, but that rule was chosen from 12, so its evidence carries the weight of 12 trials and the multiple-testing penalty applies in full. A clean test would re-run on a period or universe never seen during screening.

3. 組合構造同 v2 不同。Basket rebalance, not portfolio_test.walk. walk sizes off entry-to-stop distance and momentum has no stop. The guardrails are reimplemented here; the position-sizing engine is not shared.
